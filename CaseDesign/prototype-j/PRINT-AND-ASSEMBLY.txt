FUSE VAULT CASE J — 21 October 2026 — PHYSICAL FIT PROTOTYPE

PRINT NEW: base.stl, lid.stl, dpad.stl, and TWO copies of universal_end_cap.stl.
REUSE: your successful F Select and Back buttons and USB-C collar.
The three replacement files in REUSE-OR-PRINT are byte-identical to F.
Revision I is withdrawn: its fixed D-pad bearing could not be assembled.
Do not combine the F/I D-pad or lid with the J parts.

100% SCALE — millimetres. The case has been redesigned, not globally scaled.
Body: 77.3 x 26.2 x 10.85 mm (F: 77.3 x 27.6 x 12.1 mm).
Height with existing round buttons: 12.45 mm.
Both caps fitted: 112.4 mm overall length. Equal cap lengths deliberately
accommodate the longer connector at either end. Caps match the body width and
height, with a 0.15 mm axial seam, instead of overlapping its exterior.
USB-A opening: 6.7 mm high. SD card access is closed.

D-PAD
One rigid part inserted from below the lid. Its wider underside flange cannot
pass upward through the smaller cross opening. Four thickened roof corners
limit lift near the pivot while leaving arm ends clear to rock.
Opening clearance is 0.35 mm per side (F: 0.75 mm). CAD blocks a 0.40 mm upward
shift and permits the sampled +/-3 degree cardinal/diagonal rocking range.
Contact posts are 0.10 mm nearer the switches than F. Their tips sit at Z=3.65,
about 0.088 mm above the library switch tops. The centre heel still meets PCB
Z=1.6; its reach relative to the contact tips is reduced by 0.10 mm.
This is intentionally not a preloaded, zero-clearance fit. An opposite arm
rises when another is pressed. Actual switch travel and return need testing.
The small orientation dot faces USB-A.

CAPS
Print TWO copies of universal_end_cap.stl in TPU, using the material that
worked for your previous covers. Either cap fits either end, right-side-up.
No A/C labels are needed because the caps are identical.
Two concealed side tongues slide into matching wall channels;
small TPU bumps seat in pockets. Recessed finger grips help removal.
Do not print these caps in rigid PLA: the detents require flexibility.
No separate fasteners, glue, bearing or retaining pieces are required.

PRINT ORIENTATION
All print files are on the bed. Base: floor down. Lid: outer face down.
D-pad: thumb face down. Cap: closed end down, tongues pointing upward.
Use the same rigid material as the working F controls for the D-pad.
Suggested starting point: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.12 mm layers for lid/D-pad,
0.16 mm for base, at least 3 walls. Check slicer preview around the cap's
horizontal detent channels; these have 1 mm spans. Clean strings/burrs from
channels and D-pad opening without enlarging the bearing surfaces.
Do not scale individual parts to compensate for fit.

ASSEMBLY
1. With the lid off the board, insert D-pad, Select and Back from underneath.
   The cross passes through its opening; its flange stays beneath the roof.
   Dot toward USB-A. The loose lid does not retain controls against falling
   out underneath: support them while handling it.
2. Fit the SD card, then the PCB and original USB-C collar into the base.
3. DRY-FIT THE DISPLAY FLEX. The reduced floor reserves 2.4 mm below PCB rather
   than the earlier provisional 3.0 mm. Actual folded flex thickness is not
   measured. Arrange a gentle fold and never use the snaps to compress it.
4. Hold the controls in place, engage the USB-C lid hooks, and lower the lid
   over the board. Check free movement before fully engaging the four snaps.
5. Test all six controls with the diagnostic firmware. Every control must
   release, and none should register when untouched. If a switch remains
   pressed or the D-pad binds, stop and inspect the fit before use.
6. Slide either TPU cap straight onto either end. Grip the recessed sides to
   pull it off. Caps are removed before opening the case.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
CAD checks include solid validity, watertight meshes, 278 electronics solids,
board loading, actual underside control insertion, sampled lid closure,
D-pad rocking and positive lift/side stops, button travel, cap interchange,
rigid cap insertion/removal and SD wall continuity. TPU detent deformation is
intentional and isolated in removal checks; its force/wear is not simulated.
These are CAD checks, not a printed fit trial. Switch feel, printer tolerance,
cap retention, snap strength and the display flex remain physical checks.
