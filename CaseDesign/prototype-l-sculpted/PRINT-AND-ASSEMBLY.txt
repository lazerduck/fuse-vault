FUSE VAULT L — SCULPTED PANELS

NEW PARTS
Print the new base and multipart lid as a matching shell. An existing panelled-v2
base is geometrically equivalent and may be reused if identified confidently.
Reuse the working L PCB/display/shim assembly and collar. Existing L controls
still fit; included D-pad and round buttons add 0.20 mm easing only to the exposed
top edges. Print these if you want the complete tactile finish.
Original L caps still fit. For the new cap-face treatment, print TWO copies of
universal_end_cap.stl. Keep previous working parts until the new print is checked.

COLOUR LID
Open lid-ams.3mf as ONE multipart object in Bambu Studio:
- lid_ams_charcoal: charcoal structural carrier, logo arms and screen lip.
- lid_ams_grey: all three bevelled grey face panels.
- lid_ams_orange: logo centre and diagonal joint tongue.

Alternatively import those THREE grouped STL files together and preserve shared
placement. Do not arrange the groups separately. The model is already face down.
The other lid*.stl files are individual editable regions or the single-colour
alternative; do not add them to the grouped print. The 3MF has no printer profile.

SETTINGS TO START FROM
P1S / stock 0.4 mm nozzle. Compatible ordinary PLA colours, established filament
flow and temperatures. Lid: 0.12 mm layers, four walls, five top/bottom layers.
Use the normal first-layer setting and inspect the actual slice preview.
No global scaling, no supports in guides or colour pockets.

Face tops and perimeter rail share the bed plane. The 0.30 mm panel bevels grow
outward away from the plate, then join the structural carrier. The recessed
channels start 0.40 mm above the bed and bridge their narrow gaps. Inspect these
layers for disconnected starts or unfilled gaps with your extrusion settings.
The narrow orange tongue contacts the bed; its exposed top width is about
0.58 mm after its bevel. Confirm a continuous extrusion in the slicer preview.
Do not force a decorative detail with unsupported thin-wall settings.

Base and caps: start with L's 0.16 mm layers, four walls, five solid top/bottom
layers and 20% infill. Base underside down; caps closed end down, openings up.
The cap-face recesses are shallow and bevelled. Inspect their ends in preview.

For a quick one-colour geometry trial, use lid_single_colour.stl instead of the
multipart lid. It preserves the panel relief but not the visible flush logo.

ASSEMBLY
Preserve the existing display adhesive stack and free flex route when transferring
the working PCB. Fit card, collar and board into the base; follow L assembly notes
for a complete new electronics assembly. Insert buttons from beneath the lid.
The D-pad's orientation dot faces USB-A. Engage the USB-C hooks, close gently,
and test every control for press and release. The display must not carry closure
force. Fit the two caps in either end position; they remain reversible.

CHECK THE FIRST PRINT
Panel bevel finish, orange tongue continuity, clean logo, seam bridging, smooth
SD shoulder, screen sight lines, button return, shell and cap retention. CAD checks
do not establish adhesion, printed surface quality, fatigue or switch feel.
