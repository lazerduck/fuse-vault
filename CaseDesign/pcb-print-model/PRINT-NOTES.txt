CURRENT PCB — RIGID FIT DUMMY

Print pcb-fit-dummy.stl at 100% scale, millimetres. Flat face is already on Z=0.
Suggested starting settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16 mm layers (0.12 mm if convenient),
3 walls, 15–20% infill. No supports should be needed: housings are solid envelopes,
USB connector envelopes extend to the bed, and the underside is a solid slab.
Inspect the slicer preview to confirm that all six small actuator tops are present.
Do not scale the part to compensate for printer fit; that would move the buttons.

PCB underside is 2.05 mm above the bed. PCB top is 3.65 mm above the bed.
Nominal PCB thickness is 1.60 mm; fixed switch tops are 5.55 mm above the bed.
The 2.05 mm underside allowance comes from the current exported component models.
The export's PCB thickness was 1.5862 mm; top heights are referenced to its surface
and the printable slab uses the intended nominal 1.60 mm thickness.

This is a rigid mechanical dummy, not a functioning D-pad. It tests XY alignment,
resting contact height and room for printed button parts. It cannot test switch
travel, operating force or tactile feel. Switch actuator contact shape is simplified.
The full underside slab and filled USB envelopes deliberately occupy empty space:
this model cannot verify bottom-component clearances, case standoffs, connector
underside clearance, microSD access or the board installation path.

No display or display flex is included: those are absent from the current PCB STEP
export. Do not treat this dummy as verification of display-to-lid clearance.
Tiny drill holes/slots, leads and hollow connector interiors are deliberately omitted.
Component envelopes are based on the current EasyEDA STEP and placements. Switch
height is set to its specified 1.90 mm; L1 is set to its 2.00 mm maximum. Other heights
remain dependent on the accuracy of the EasyEDA library models and seating.
