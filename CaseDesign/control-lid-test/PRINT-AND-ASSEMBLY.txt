CONTROL-AREA LID TEST — FOR THE CURRENT PRINTED PCB DUMMY

Print one each:
  lid-section.stl
  dpad.stl
  select.stl
  back.stl
Select and Back are the same geometry; you may instead print two copies of either.

All STLs are already bed-oriented. Do not auto-orient them:
- Lid: exterior/top face on the bed, side rails and guides pointing up.
- D-pad and buttons: thumb faces on the bed, stems pointing up.
Print at 100% scale in mm, with your 0.4 mm nozzle, preferably 0.12–0.16 mm
layers, 3 walls and 20% infill. Supports should not be needed. Leave the mating
surfaces free of supports. Use elephant-foot compensation if normally calibrated;
do not enlarge/shrink the entire part. Check that the slicer preserves the thin
button guide walls and the four D-pad contact posts. A small brim can help the
separate buttons; remove it completely before assembly.

Assembly:
1. Clear strings and rough edges, especially the cross opening and round guides.
2. Hold the lid upside down. Insert the two buttons from inside, their small round
   faces through the round holes and their collars remaining beneath the lid.
3. Insert the D-pad from inside. Its small engraved dot must point toward USB-A,
   the large connector at the end of the dummy. Its four offset posts must sit
   over their corresponding switch tops; do not rotate it by 90 or 180 degrees.
4. Keep the loose controls in place and lower the fixture over the dummy. Its two
   side rails straddle the long PCB edges and stand on the same flat table as
   the dummy. The small inner end stop meets the PCB-facing side of USB-A.
   Gently slide it toward USB-A until that stop touches; no force is needed.
5. Hold the fixture down during testing. It is a removable bridge, not a snap-fit
   shell. The controls are captive while the fixture sits on the dummy, but may
   fall out from underneath when it is lifted.

What to check:
- Both buttons slide without binding and land on the middle of their switch tops.
- The D-pad sits on its rounded central heel, aligns with all four switches, and
  has a little play before touching a switch. Its hidden skirt must not rub the
  two button guides or catch in the cross opening.
- The fixture sits flat and its rails fit around the dummy without squeezing it.

The dummy switches are RIGID. Expect only the initial free play (nominally 0.25 mm
at each plunger), not a click or spring return. Do not force full travel against
the dummy: it cannot simulate switch compression. The approximately 6-degree CAD
motion check is a guide-clearance test for future real-switch use, not available
motion on the rigid dummy. This print does not qualify operating force, overtravel
protection or retention in a finished enclosure.

The 1.4 mm roof and 10 mm fixture height are test dimensions, not a finished-case
height commitment. The display, full enclosure, microSD access and real underside
supports are not tested by this control-area fixture.

Validation: four single-body watertight STLs; no nominal resting intersections
between the parts or the dummy. Sampled +/-6-degree cardinal/diagonal D-pad tilt
clears the lid and separate controls; buttons clear the lid for 0.6 mm depression.
All fit/tolerance and print-strength claims still require the physical test.
