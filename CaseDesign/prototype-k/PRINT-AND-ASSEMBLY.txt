FUSE VAULT CASE K — 21 October 2026 — ALL PARTS PLA

PRINT NEW: base, lid, D-pad, Select, Back, and TWO universal end caps.
REUSE: the existing USB-C collar only.
Use revision K parts together. F/J round buttons are too tall for this lid.
This replaces J following physical feedback; CAD checks are not a print trial.

CHANGES
D-pad: four 3.2 mm diameter pads instead of 1.7 mm posts, about 3.5 times the
contact area. Pads merge into the underside skirt; no thin projecting stems.
Their contact height remains Z=3.65. The small orientation dot faces USB-A.
The simple underside flange and close lid opening from J remain.

Round buttons: exposed tops trimmed by 0.55 mm, restoring 1.05 mm protrusion
above the lid, as in F. Stems, retaining flanges and seated positions unchanged.
Do not scale the parts or shorten their switch-contact stems.

SD: restored side shoulder and installed-card allowance out to Y=2.5, with a
continuous outer wall at Y=2.5..3.9. Floor restored to Z=-2.9; base bottom -4.1
keeps a flat printable floor 1.2 mm thick. The enclosure is closed around this
space; it is not filled in. Card/socket is loaded from above before the lid.
The reserved loading volume is X=50.7..68.1, Y=-15..2.5, Z=-2.7 upwards.
This combines the earlier F clearance with the current socket width. It is not
an assertion that the fitted card has been physically measured anew.

Caps: return to full overlapping sleeves with 1.6 mm nominal walls, not the
thin-tongue J design. Print TWO copies of universal_end_cap.stl in PLA.
Both caps are identical and fit either end, right-side-up or upside-down.
The small bumps are at the shell centre height, Z=1.575, on both sides.
They seat in shallow dimples in the case. Nominal interference is only 0.10 mm
per side; fit, retention force and durability still depend on the actual print.
The stops are symmetric too. No A/C labels. Caps overlap the body instead of sitting flush with it.

DIMENSIONS
Main body: 77.3 x 26.2 x 11.35 mm.
Width at the restored SD shoulder: 27.6 mm.
Body height with round buttons: 12.4 mm. Round buttons stand 1.05 mm above lid.
Both end caps fitted: overall bounds 110.8 x 29.8 x 14.95 mm.
Equal caps need enough depth for USB-A, so the USB-C cap has additional room.

PRINT
All files are millimetres, oriented on the bed. Print at 100% scale in PLA.
Base: flat underside down. Lid and three controls: outside/thumb faces down.
Caps: closed end on bed, opening up. Use the same PLA settings as your prior
successful parts. For a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.12 mm layers on the controls/lid and
0.16 mm on the base/caps are starting points; use four walls where they fit.
Check the slicer around the sleeve's internal stop ramps and the lid guides.
Remove strings and elephant-foot burrs. Do not enlarge the D-pad opening or
sand its retaining flange indiscriminately. Inspect cap bumps before fitting.

ASSEMBLY
1. Fit the SD card into its socket. Fit the PCB and original USB-C collar into
   the base from above. Confirm the fitted card clears the restored shoulder.
2. Dry-fit the display and its folded cable; restored floor allowance is not a
   substitute for checking the actual fold. Do not use the lid to compress it.
3. Insert the new K Select, Back and D-pad through the underside of the loose
   lid. D-pad dot faces USB-A. Support controls while positioning the lid.
4. Engage the USB-C hooks, lower the lid gently and check movement before
   fully engaging the four snaps. No switch should be pressed at rest.
5. Test all six switches individually with the diagnostic firmware. Confirm
   each releases and inspect Up/Left feel with the broader D-pad contact pads.
6. Fit either cap to either end. Either 180-degree roll orientation works.
   If a PLA cap needs excessive force, remove it and inspect bumps/burrs;
   do not force the sleeve onto the connector. Retention needs a physical trial.
   Remove both caps before opening the case.

VALIDATION
Checks cover valid solids and watertight meshes, the 278 exported electronics
solids, explicit installed-card and loading reserves, closed SD walls/floor,
D-pad +/-3-degree rocking, underside control insertion, sampled lid closure,
unchanged button geometry below the trimmed tops, matching cap geometry and
180-degree roll symmetry, cap removal excluding only intentional bump flex.
Broad pad faces stay above a Z=3.2 housing-check plane in the sampled motion;
nominal switch housing top is Z=3.0. This does not simulate actuator compression
or guarantee the electrical click at every tolerance extreme.
All mechanical feel, PLA retention/wear and actual card/flex fit remain print
checks. No claim is made that revision K has already been physically tested.
