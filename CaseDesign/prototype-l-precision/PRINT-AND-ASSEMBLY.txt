Use the existing working L internals, base, buttons, D-pad, screen shim and collar.
Do not disturb the bonded display to try a cosmetic lid. Follow L assembly notes
if building a complete second device. All full-set parts are included for that use.

NEW PRINTS
- Lid: load lid-ams.3mf as one multipart object in Bambu Studio.
- Assign lid to slate grey, lid_inlay_dark to charcoal,
  lid_inlay_orange to orange. Print Select orange and D-pad/Back charcoal if desired.
- Print TWO universal_end_cap.stl for the grooved precision caps.

P1S / 0.4 mm nozzle / compatible PLA colours from the same material family.
Use the established L settings; 0.12 mm layers for the lid, four walls, five
solid top/bottom layers. Inlays extend 0.36 mm from the face. This is a model,
not a pre-sliced printer project. Check the preview and actual first-layer height.

The lid and colour bodies already share a face-down print transform. Preserve
their registration; do not auto-arrange them separately. The three STL colour
bodies must all be present. lid.stl by itself contains empty colour pockets.
Use lid_single_colour.stl for a one-colour alternative with no visible logo.

The panel seam, if present, is 0.24 mm deep. With the lid face down, its floor
bridges a narrow groove; inspect first layers and do not put supports in it.
The precision cap channels are 0.24 mm deep, 1 mm wide, on both broad faces.
Caps print closed end down, opening up, as in L; inspect the channel ends in
preview. No supports should occupy detents, guides or inlays.

Dry-fit the lid and check every control presses and releases. Keep the D-pad's
orientation dot facing USB-A. Use the existing L shell snaps without forcing
closure. Check cap retention and texture on a physical print. Shallow cosmetic
cuts retain CAD backing, but force, wear and multi-colour bonding are untested.
