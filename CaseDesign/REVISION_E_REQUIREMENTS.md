# Prototype E requirements

D physical feedback: latch failed to catch and was too flimsy. Slimmer dimensions,
SD bay and end caps were positively received. Printer has a 0.4 mm nozzle.

User direction: use small sloping snaps on shell-alignment features, with shallow
matching recesses. Moderate strength is required because the closed shell secures
the USB-C collar. Opening is occasional; hundreds of service cycles are unnecessary.
The end covers must not be the only mechanism holding the shell closed.

E implements four short supported snap posts, retains the original USB-C hooks,
and removes D's isolated spring arm and support web. Standard engagement is 0.25 mm;
0.15 and 0.35 mm fit samples are also included. Board supports remain independent.
Only the base/lid need reprinting. D body envelope, controls, collar and covers
are retained. CAD clearance/capture checks are not a physical strength result.
