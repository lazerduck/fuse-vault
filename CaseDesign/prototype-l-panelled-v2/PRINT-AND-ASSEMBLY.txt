FUSE VAULT L — PANELLED V2 / SMOOTH SD SHOULDER

Print a NEW BASE and NEW LID as a matching pair. Reuse the working L controls,
shim, collar and caps. The cap geometry, PCB locators, screen reserve, guides,
D-pad contacts and shell snaps are unchanged. The new exterior curve is present
on both shell halves. Do not mix old and new halves for the intended finish.

LID
Open lid-ams.3mf as ONE multipart object in Bambu Studio.
Assign lid = light/cool grey, lid_inlay_dark = charcoal,
lid_inlay_orange = orange. All bodies share a face-down print orientation.
Do not arrange them separately. Equivalent multipart STEP and STL files included.
This is a model, not a pre-sliced P1S project.

P1S / 0.4 mm nozzle, compatible PLA colours, established filament profiles.
Start from L settings: 0.12 mm lid layers, four walls, five top/bottom layers.
Inspect first layers: logo/perimeter colours extend 0.36 mm into the face;
the screen surround colour extends through the visible bevel, 0.85 mm deep.
The panel joint is 0.24 mm deep and bridges across a narrow face-down recess.
Its orange inset is recessed too. Avoid support inside colour regions/guides.
Use the actual build-plate and filament settings from your working L prints.

The base is underside down. Start with L's 0.16 mm layers and 20% infill.
The exterior blend retains L's 0.55 mm top/bottom edge easing. Inspect the layer
preview at the blend and snaps. Do not globally scale parts to adjust fit.

FILES
lid.stl alone is incomplete: use it with both lid_inlay_*.stl files.
lid_single_colour.stl is complete for a one-colour fit print, but has no visible
logo or coloured perimeter. Do not print it on top of the multipart version.
The full-set control/cap/shim/collar STLs are included only if replacements are
needed. universal_end_cap.stl is identical to the working L cap; print twice.

ASSEMBLY
Transfer the existing PCB, collar, card and display/shim assembly carefully.
Preserve the bonded display height and free flex route; use L assembly notes
if building a complete second device. Load the D-pad from beneath the lid with
its dot toward USB-A. Fit both round buttons; engage the USB-C hooks and close
the shell gently. Check every direction and button presses and releases.
The screen must not carry shell-closing force. Fit the unchanged L caps.

WHAT TO CHECK ON THE FIRST PRINT
Smooth feel at the SD shoulder and seam between the new halves; clean logo and
recessed joint; screen readability; control return; shell and cap retention.
CAD checks pass but finish, material bonding and long-term wear are untested.
