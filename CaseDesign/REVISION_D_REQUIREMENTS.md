# V2 prototype D — physical-use feedback

Accepted direction:

- Remove excessive board play while preserving the successful open loading path
  and enlarged SD-card notch. Use short locators instead of a tight enclosed groove.
- Keep hooks at USB-C and use a snap/latch near USB-A in place of the screw/nut.
- Reduce excess dimensions throughout the enclosure, preserving room for real
  components, assembly and the display flex. The user reports that its small cable
  bulge can settle lower easily; this is not permission to crease or clamp it.
- Improve end-cover retention. Chosen first trial: rounded TPU ribs engaging
  recesses in the case, keeping retention forces off the USB connector shells.
- Preserve the successful back-button flexure concept, but mark its press point.
- Omit the joystick cap entirely; use the bare plastic shaft.

Prototype D targets a 12.1 mm body instead of 13.8 mm. Its maximum width is
27.6 mm instead of 30.6 mm, retaining a local SD bay; most of its length is 26.2 mm
wide. Local cable depth is 3.0 mm, reduced from the previous provisional 3.5 mm.
The back leaf retains its thickness and switch-tip height but has a changed roof
transition and tactile recess, so its action must be checked again.

The new latch and cap detents require physical trials. Latch coupons are included.
See `prototype-d/README.md` and `validation.json` for assembly and limitations.
