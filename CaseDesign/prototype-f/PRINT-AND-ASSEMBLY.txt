FUSE VAULT — FULL CASE F, D-PAD PCB

Print new BASE and LID. These supersede the E shell halves and should be used
as a pair. The full case is designed around the actual populated PCB, not the
solid-underneath printed dummy.

REUSE:
- The D-pad, Select and Back parts from your successful control-area test.
  Geometrically identical replacements are included in this pack.
- Prototype E USB-C collar and both USB end covers. They are unchanged and
  are included for convenience. Keep the end covers in the same material
  used successfully before (their detent ribs were designed for TPU).

PRINT:
All STL files are in mm and already oriented with their intended bed face down.
Use 100% scale. Do not auto-orient or scale to fit.
For base/lid: start with a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16 mm layers, 3 walls, 20% infill.
For the controls: 0.12–0.16 mm layers. Lid and controls print outside face down.
Inspect slicer paths at the button guides and snap posts. Keep supports away
from the guide bores and snap mating surfaces. The top has a flat print face;
local internal bridges/overhangs still need checking in your slicer, particularly
around the USB-C hooks and underside access holes.

CHANGES FROM E:
- Old joystick hole and integral Back flexure replaced by the tested D-pad
  opening and two collared, guided buttons at the current switch positions.
- Tested control geometry retained. Roof underside locally at Z=6.55 mm,
  exterior at Z=7.8 mm, giving 1.25 mm roof thickness around the controls.
- SD access widened toward USB-A; the nearby upper support shifted to X=69 mm.
- USB-A opening lowered to clear the current connector library model beneath
  the PCB. Existing collar capture, snap design, screen window and outer body
  envelope are retained. Body thickness remains 12.1 mm, excluding controls.
- Local button-guide clearance provided for the previous display envelope.

ASSEMBLY:
1. Remove strings/elephant foot from mating edges, guide bores and controls.
2. Fit the existing USB-C collar to the connector. Lower the populated PCB into
   the base, sitting on its support ledges. Check that no parts are pressed by
   a support. Keep the display flex in its existing clearance pocket.
3. With the lid upside down, insert Select and Back from underneath, collars
   inside. Insert the D-pad with its small dot facing USB-A. Its asymmetric
   underside contacts must align with the four switches.
4. Support the loose controls while bringing the lid to the case. Engage the
   USB-C-end hooks and lower the USB-A end gently until the snaps seat.
   Do not force a binding control or trapped flex. Try the controls before
   fitting the end covers; they must release freely on real switches.
5. Check the SD card can be pushed/released and removed through the widened
   side opening. The card's exact ejection projection/finger access needs the
   real socket/card test; the CAD check reserves a clear withdrawal corridor.

LIMITATIONS / WHAT THIS PRINT TESTS:
- The control-area print was successful. Full-shell fit and snap holding force
  remain a physical test, as do real button force, return and overtravel.
- The printed PCB dummy has a flat slab underneath that occupies the support
  spaces. It is NOT expected to fit the new base correctly. Do not file away
  the base's real PCB supports merely to fit that dummy.
- The screen window and provisional display/flex envelope come from E. They
  have not been remeasured on the new hardware. This model does not claim
  verified glass, adhesive or flex thickness.
- Internal checks use the current saved EasyEDA 3D export. Library model
  accuracy and manufacturing/printing tolerances still apply.
- Keep the previously printed E shell if needed; earlier models are unchanged.
