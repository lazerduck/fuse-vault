16 mm D-pad packaging study — 12 October 2026

Result
The concept fits the existing Prototype E external body (77.3 x 27.6 x
12.1 mm). It requires a new lid and PCB layout; it is not a replacement
cap for the existing five-way switch. The current base is used unchanged
for this packaging study. Its suitability for changed underside routing
or solder features is not established.

Controls
D-pad centre: (61.100, -11.800), versus SW3 (60.325, -14.732).
The shift is +0.775 mm X and +2.932 mm Y.
Visible pad: 16 mm across, arms 5.5 mm wide, with 0.3 mm top bevel.
Lid opening: 17.2 mm across, arms 6.7 mm wide.
Hidden retaining disc: diameter 14.6 mm; pocket diameter 15.8 mm.
Select and Back heads: diameter 4.4 mm, 9.675 mm centre spacing.
Back remains over SW4; Select is above it. The original long Back leaf
is replaced by two independently guided captive plungers.
The exposed pad and Back head have approximately 2 mm closest plan gap.
These are compact controls; thumb comfort has not been demonstrated.

Height
PCB top Z=1.6; case top Z=7.8; pad and button tops Z=8.4.
The pad is 0.6 mm proud of the lid at rest and 6.8 mm above the PCB.
This corrects the earlier generic 3–5 mm PCB-to-pad estimate: on this
existing case the roof position sets the control height.
Overall height from case bottom: 12.7 mm at rest, versus 14.9 mm for the
existing bare joystick. See measurements.json for the raised-edge height
at the checked +/-4 degree tilts (approximately 13.2 mm).
USB end covers are excluded from these control-height comparisons.

PCB space
Four representative KMR2 switches sit 5.5 mm from the pad centre.
Body envelopes are 4.2 x 2.8 mm, with actuator height 1.9 mm above PCB.
Each land reserve is 5 x 3.2 mm; the four direction reserves fit inside
14.2 x 14.2 mm, or 14.8 x 14.8 mm with 0.3 mm exterior margin.
The previous SW3 terminal reserve was provisionally 13.3 x 10.5 mm;
thus the new directional land bounding box adds 0.9 x 3.7 mm locally.
Select needs an additional 5 x 3.2 mm reserve alongside the pad.
The up-switch reserve/courtyard clashes with R19, R20, R22 and R23.
Those parts are omitted from the proposed assembly to show a revised
layout. They are not deleted electrically; their new locations and
routing still need design. SW3 is also omitted. No original PCB or CAD
files have been edited.
Part source: Littelfuse C&K KMR2 series datasheet:
https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/littelfuse-ck-tactile-kmr2-series-datasheet?assetguid=f782bfc7-b600-4ab5-8c8d-a2a094a642e3
The switch CAD is a simplified study envelope, not final vendor CAD.
Verify the chosen orderable part, land pattern, stroke, force and contact
rating before board manufacture.

Mechanism and execution
The cross, flange, central rounded heel and four contact plungers form
one printed part. The heel bears against a clear flat soldermask area on
the PCB, so there is no centre switch or hole into the underside SD area.
The flange catches beneath the lid, in the diagonal areas outside the
cross aperture. The square cross opening restrains rotation. The roof
remaining over the pocket is 0.75 mm nominal, so it needs a print/strength
check. PCB support, soldermask wear and surface tolerances also matter.
Switch plungers have 0.15 mm nominal neutral clearance. This avoids
assuming exact preload, but could produce a loose feel or poor centring.
Real prototypes must tune this clearance, return/preload and travel stops.
Hard stops are not yet engineered in this study: do not print and use it
as an operational final lid. A small coupon is the next useful step.

Checks
CAD solids are valid and connected. No intersections were found between
the pad and the modelled lid, base, separate buttons or retained component
solids in neutral or nine sampled angles from -4 to +4 degrees about each
cardinal axis. Intentional compression of new switch actuators is not a
collision failure. These are discrete rigid-body samples, not a tolerance,
stress, continuous-sweep or switch-internals analysis.
No full diagonal, retention/lift-out, moving-button, assembly-insertion,
USB-cap-motion or physical ergonomic qualification was done.
Existing electronics retain the baseline model's provisional dimensions.
All baseline Prototype E files are hash-checked unchanged by the script.
